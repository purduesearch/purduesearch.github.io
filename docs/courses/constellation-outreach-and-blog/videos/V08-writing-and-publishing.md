## Purpose

Two things a new author gets wrong: they don't realise other people are editing the same document
live, and they don't realise publishing puts something on the public internet under the club's name.

## Narration

**[00:00 — blog list]**

The club blog is the public face of what we do. Sponsors read it. Prospective members read it.
Sometimes journalists read it.

*(pause)*

It's written here.

**[00:20 — two cursors]**

First thing to understand: this editor is live. That's another person's cursor.

*(beat)*

You're both in the same document at the same time, like a Google Doc. There is no "send me your
version." There is no merge. It just works, and it means the reviewing and the writing can happen
together instead of in sequence.

**[00:52 — toolbar, callout]**

Headings, quotes, callouts, images, tables. Stick to the built-in styles rather than hand-formatting —
these are what the public site knows how to render, and a hand-styled paragraph will look wrong on a
phone.

**[01:24 — image insert]**

Images get optimised on upload — resized, compressed, converted. You don't have to prepare anything.
Drop the full-size photo in.

**[01:52 — AI review]**

There's a review panel that reads the draft and suggests structure, clarity, and headline
alternatives.

*(pause)*

Treat it like a second reader with opinions. Some of them are good. It doesn't know that the rover
is named after someone's dog, and it will confidently tell you to cut that.

**[02:20 — publish / schedule]**

Then publishing. You can publish now, or schedule it — Constellation checks every five minutes and
puts it live at the time you set.

*(beat)*

And be clear about what that button does. It puts this on the public internet, under the club's name,
where anyone can read it. Which is the point. But it's worth one more read before you press it.

**[02:52 — public site]**

Here it is on the live site. Same content, rendered by the public renderer.

**[03:20 — editor]**

Next you'll draft one and take it to the edge of publishing.

*(hold, fade)*

## Notes for the recorder

- The two-cursor shot must be genuinely simultaneous — two browsers, both typing. A cut between them
  loses the entire point, which is that collaboration is live and requires no coordination.
- The "named after someone's dog" line does real work: it tells authors when to overrule the AI
  without saying "ignore the AI."
- Do **not** actually publish during the recording. Use an already-published demo post for the public
  site shot.
