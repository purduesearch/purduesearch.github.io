# Your skills, backed by your work

Open **Skills** in the desktop sidebar, or **More → Skills** on a phone. Your Profile has a short summary linking to the same workspace.

**My skills** shows what the available evidence suggests. Open a skill to inspect the work, contribution, outcome and source. Confidence and proficiency are different. Self-claims, peers, leads and analysis remain separate statements. You can question an inference and choose teammate and public sharing independently. Newly discovered skills start privately; a portfolio stays off until you enable it.

In **Growth**, choose up to three focus skills. Suggestions come from accessible project tasks that explicitly mention those concepts or their aliases. Save an opportunity or open its task. Neither action automatically assigns you work. Describe fabrication, mentoring or outreach work with **Add work or context** when the digital record is incomplete.

**Team coverage** shows shared evidence in a project you belong to. Unknown means there is not enough shared evidence. Ask someone about availability and willingness before proposing a pairing.

The **Library** explains definitions and other names for a skill. New supported concepts can be created automatically. Admins use **Automation** to inspect exceptions, run health and the change history. Original evidence and human statements remain available after consolidation.
