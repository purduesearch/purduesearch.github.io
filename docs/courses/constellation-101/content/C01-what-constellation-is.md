## Welcome

Constellation is SEARCH's project system. It's where the club's work is planned, tracked, discussed,
and recorded — and it's the answer to the question "what's the actual status of this?"

You're about to spend roughly 50 minutes learning it. That's a real ask, so here's the honest return:
you'll stop losing work in chat threads, you'll stop being the person who didn't know a deadline
moved, and the hours you put into this club will be visible to the people writing sponsorship reports
and recommendation letters.

## The one idea

**Work has a state, and the state lives in one place.**

Not in someone's memory. Not in a Slack thread from three weeks ago. Not in a Drive folder that four
people have a different version of. In Constellation, on a board, where anyone can look at it without
asking anyone.

Everything else in this course is a consequence of that idea.

## How it fits with what you already use

> **Constellation doesn't replace your tools. It connects them.**

| Tool | Still used for | What Constellation adds |
|---|---|---|
| **Slack** | All conversation | DMs you when something needs you; posts milestones and completions to project channels |
| **Google Drive** | Documents, CAD, media | Links files to the task or project they belong to |
| **GitHub** | Code | Links pull requests to tasks; can block "done" on failing checks |

If you take one thing from this page: you don't have to remember to check Constellation. Set your
notification preferences once, and it comes and finds you in Slack.

## About the hands-on parts

Several sections are **walkthroughs**: Constellation takes over your screen, dims everything that
isn't relevant, and points at exactly where to go. Some of them ask you to actually do things —
create a task, drag a card, log time.

> **Nothing you do in this course touches real club data.**
> The moment a walkthrough needs somewhere to practice, Constellation sets up a private training
> project that belongs only to you. It's hidden from every dashboard, leaderboard, and report in the
> product, it grants no real XP, and it's archived when you finish. Click freely.
